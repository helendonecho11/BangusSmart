A photo cannot reveal exact age. This tool measures visible geometry under the capture protocol below. All media-derived age estimates have low, uncalibrated confidence. Output assists farm personnel and does not replace their judgment.

Before you capture

01 · Prepare the fish

Use a straight side view in a shallow light tray or clear tank, with a plain background and even lighting. Separate fish; show the snout and complete forked tail.

02 · Include the scale

Put an 8 × 6 square checkerboard in the same plane as the fish. Measure a square in cm. A ruler alone is not supported by this first implementation.

03 · Keep the camera square

Look straight down at the board and fish. Avoid glare, blur, bending and overlap. Keep camera and board stationary for videos.
