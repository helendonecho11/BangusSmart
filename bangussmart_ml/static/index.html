How age is determined

  1. Known hatch date: observation date minus hatch date.
  2. Known stocking date / day: days of culture. Age at stocking remains unknown.
  3. Measured weight only: match weight against the existing synthetic growth model over days 0–147, at the supplied mean temperature (26.5–31.5 °C).
  4. Photo only: age unavailable. No trained vision model exists in this prototype.

No real-fish age accuracy has been measured. Matching day ranges reflect model ties and are not confidence intervals.
